# What are the KPI for Executive Coaching?

July 24, 2023

![writing women's career stories for Madam Ambition](/articles/images/business-coach-bookings.jpg "business-coach-bookings")

KPI for Executive Coaching: How do you know that Executive Coaching is working for you? Aside from enjoying your time in each session, how can you track your progress so that you know there is a good return on investment in your coaching relationship? Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) for an executive coach are essential metrics that measure the effectiveness and impact of their coaching services. These indicators help assess the coach’s performance and their client’s progress. Here are some key performance indicators for an executive coach:

1. **Client Satisfaction:** Measuring client satisfaction through feedback surveys or testimonials provides insights into how well the executive coach meets their client’s needs and expectations. A high level of client satisfaction indicates successful coaching engagements. You should feel good after each session. You should be motivated and have clarity about the next steps. You should feel the success coming with your work improving.
2. **Client Goal Achievement:** Tracking your goals while starting and whatever other goals come up. You need to be able to see progress in meaningful ways in your professional development. Goals can change, but progress is essential to a good coaching/coachee relationship.
3. **Improved Leadership Competencies:** Build on the skills you have. Learn to build the skills of your team. Assessing the enhancement of specific leadership competencies, such as communication, decision-making, emotional intelligence, and strategic thinking, showcases the coach’s impact on developing their clients’ skills. Whatever skill you are working on, ensure a clear goal is set for where you want to be.
4. **Business Impact:** Measuring the impact of coaching on the clients’ professional performance, such as increased productivity, improved team dynamics, and enhanced overall leadership effectiveness, demonstrates the value the coach brings to the organization.
5. **Return on Investment (ROI):** Evaluating the coaching engagement’s financial return or cost-benefit ratio helps clients and organizations understand the tangible benefits gained from working with the executive coach.

To understand KPI for Executive Coaching fully, it’s important to note that while they offer valuable insights, the effectiveness of coaching can also be subjective and difficult to measure in quantifiable terms. Therefore, combining qualitative feedback and success stories with these KPIs provides a more comprehensive evaluation of an executive coach’s performance.
